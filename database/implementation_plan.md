Category 1: The Connectivity Layer (NOBODY is talking about this)
Every CPO module must physically connect to fiber. The connectors and cables between optics and the network rack are a completely unrepresented category in our lists.

Why it's hidden in plain sight: Amphenol holds ~33% of all AI data center interconnect market share. They are the primary connector supplier for NVIDIA's NVL72/Rubin racks. With the CommScope CCS acquisition ($10.5B, completed Jan 2026), they now own the fiber AND copper infrastructure feeding CPO switches. Q1 2026 guidance: $6.9-$7.0B revenue. This is the "roads and bridges" of CPO.
Risk: Large-cap, well-known. But the CPO angle is underappreciated.
[NEW] Credo Technology (NASDAQ: CRDO) — USA, ~$12B market cap

THE 300% REVENUE PLAY: Credo just reported Q3 FY2026 revenue of $407M, up 201.5% YoY. They make the high-speed SerDes chips and Active Electrical Cables (AECs) that sit RIGHT NEXT to CPO engines. Their new ZeroFlap optics and OmniConnect gearboxes are the interface between copper and optics in AI racks. Revenue is projected to grow 50%+ again in FY2027. This is the single highest organic revenue growth rate in the entire CPO ecosystem right now.

Category 3: The Precision Dispensing Monopoly
Every single CPO assembly line requires microscopic amounts of UV-cure epoxy, underfill, and thermal interface material to be dispensed with extreme accuracy. Miss by a micron and the optics fail.

[NEW] Nordson (NASDAQ: NDSN) — USA, ~$14B market cap
Their ASYMTEK Vantage platform and IntelliJet jetting systems are the STANDARD for underfill dispensing in advanced packaging. Their MARCH plasma treatment systems prepare surfaces for die attach. As ASE builds 6 new CPO plants, every single assembly line will have Nordson dispensing equipment. Nobody in the CPO community has mentioned this company.
Category 4: The Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD) Bottleneck
Silicon photonics waveguides require ultra-thin cladding layers deposited with atomic precision. Without ALD, the light leaks out of the waveguide and the entire CPO engine fails.

[NEW] ASM International (ASMI.AS / OTC: ASMIY) — Netherlands, ~$25B market cap
The global leader in single-wafer ALD. Their equipment deposits the precise oxide/nitride layers that form waveguide cladding in SiPh chips. As photonics goes from R&D to mass production, ALD intensity increases dramatically. New Arizona fab capacity ramping H2 2026.
Category 5: Advanced Packaging Inspection (The one we missed)
[NEW] Onto Innovation (NASDAQ: ONTO) — USA, ~$8B market cap
ANOTHER 300% REVENUE CATEGORY PLAY: Their Dragonfly G5 system is specifically optimized for AI/HBM packaging. They just secured a $240M+ multi-year purchase agreement with a leading HBM customer. Advanced packaging segment revenue projected to grow 30%+ in 2026 alone, setting a record. 2.5D packaging orders more than doubled in Q4 2025. This is a direct inspection bottleneck we completely failed to include.
Category 6: The MBE Alternative (Tier-4 and TINY)
MOCVD is one way to grow InP lasers. MBE (Molecular Beam Epitaxy) is the other. It produces higher-purity crystal structures required for quantum-dot lasers used in next-gen datacom.

[NEW] Riber (ALRIB.PA) — France, ~€80M market cap
THE ULTIMATE MICRO-CAP MOONSHOT: Riber makes the only industrial-scale MBE machines on Earth. In early 2026, they announced new industrial orders specifically for scaling quantum-dot laser production for the Datacom sector. At ~€9/share and ~€80M market cap, a single hyperscaler contract could cause a 5x–10x revenue inflection. The community doesn't even know this company exists.
Category 7: Thin-Film Optical Materials
[NEW] Materion (NYSE: MTRN) — USA, ~$3.5B market cap
Materion supplies the actual sputtering targets and evaporation materials used in PVD processes that create thin-film optical coatings on SiPh chips. They cover 70%+ of the periodic table in high-purity targets. Every waveguide, every anti-reflection coating, every metal layer on a CPO chip uses their materials.
Category 8: Precision Motion for Optical Alignment
[NEW] Novanta (NASDAQ: NOVT) — USA, ~$6B market cap
Through Celera Motion, Novanta makes the sub-nanometer precision motion stages required for active optical alignment in CPO assembly. When ASMPT or Mycronic bonders need to place a laser onto a waveguide with 0.1μm accuracy, Novanta's piezo stages and encoders provide the positioning. The arms dealer's arms dealer's arms dealer.
